# 🍽️ DineSight

**DineSight** is a smart restaurant management and analytics application built in Python.  
Originally derived from **Flippify**, DineSight is repurposed to help restaurant owners streamline their business operations — from tracking sales and managing inventory to generating AI-driven insights for better decision-making.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 💼 Business Management
- **Inventory Tracking** – Monitor stock levels and avoid shortages or waste.  
- **Sales Recording** – Log daily transactions and compute totals automatically.  
- **Customer Orders** – Manage dine-in and takeout orders with ease.

### 🤖 AI Insights
- **Sales Forecasting** – Use AI to predict daily and weekly sales trends.  
- **Menu Optimization** – Identify best-selling dishes and low-performing items.  
- **Profitability Analysis** – Get data-driven insights on which items yield the most profit.

### 📊 Data Visualization
- Interactive charts and graphs using **matplotlib** for quick business overviews.  
- Exportable reports for monthly and annual performance reviews.
